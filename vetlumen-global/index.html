Science & Validation

From Light to Evidence

The validation framework is planned to separate manufacturer specifications from independently measured performance and later evidence generation.

01 · Spectral characterizationConfirm emitted wavelengths independently.
02 · Irradiance mappingMeasure delivered optical power across the active area.
03 · UniformityUnderstand spatial distribution and consistency.
04 · Thermal behaviorEvaluate temperature over intended use conditions.
05 · Safety & usabilityAssess practical interaction with animals and caregivers.
06 · Evidence generationAdvance only after engineering and safety questions are addressed.

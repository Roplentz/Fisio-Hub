Technology Philosophy

Designed to be measured before it is marketed.

We separate manufacturer specifications from independently measured product performance. The goal is disciplined translation: concept, engineering, bench validation, safety and usability, then pilot-market learning.

No efficacy, certification or regulatory approval is claimed for the current concept.

Development discipline

Designed in Brazil. Engineered through science. Built for global scale.

VETLUMEN combines Brazilian health-science and innovation capabilities with qualified OEM/ODM manufacturing. We begin with samples and pilot batches, validate the fundamentals, and scale after evidence and market learning.

Science & Validation

From Light to Evidence

A staged validation framework designed to reduce technical uncertainty before claims, commercialization or scale.

01Spectral characterization

Independent confirmation of emitted wavelengths.

02Irradiance mapping

Optical power measurement across the active area.

03Uniformity

Assessment of spatial distribution and consistency.

04Thermal behavior

Temperature profile under intended use conditions.

05Safety & usability

Practical interaction with animals and caregivers.

06Evidence generation

Advance only after engineering and safety questions are addressed.
